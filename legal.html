Privacy policy

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Fermata at tryfermata.app ("Fermata", "we", "us"). We built Fermata to collect as little as possible.

1. What you send us: sheet music

When you take a photo or choose a picture or PDF, it's sent over an encrypted connection to read the music. Before it leaves your device, the app crops the photo to the music. Then:

  • Our music reader (running on Google Cloud in the United States) reads the notes.
  • If our reader can't read the page, the page is sent to an AI service, Google Gemini or Groq, to read it instead. These services process it under their own terms. On free plans Google may use content sent to Gemini to improve its services.
  • The reading (the notes, plus the page image so it can light up the notes) is sent back to your device.
  • Our servers delete your page and everything made from it as soon as the reading is finished. We don't keep your music and we don't look at it.

Please don't upload pages with personal information written on them, and only upload music you have the right to use (see the Terms below).

2. Your microphone

The tuner, warm-ups and "sing this note" use your microphone to hear the pitch you're singing. This happens entirely on your device. Your voice is never recorded to a file, never uploaded, and never stored. You can turn microphone access off at any time in your browser or phone settings.

3. What stays on your device

  • Recent readings: your last three readings (the music and page images) are saved in your browser so you can reopen them. Remove one with its × button, or clear your browser's site data to remove them all.
  • Settings: small preferences such as your voice range for warm-ups.

These never leave your device, and we can't see them.

4. Anonymous analytics

To understand how Fermata is used and fix what breaks, we count a few things. Our analytics:

  • use no cookies and no tracking across other sites;
  • count visits and which features are used (for example "opened the tuner" or "a reading failed");
  • note the country (worked out from your connection, which is then discarded), the type of device (phone, tablet or computer) and the website that linked you (just its name, such as instagram.com);
  • count unique visitors with a code that is made from your connection details and changes every day, so it can't identify you or link your visits across days. Your IP address itself is never stored.

When a reading fails we keep a short note of the error message and how long it took, never the page itself. Analytics are kept for up to 13 months.

6. What we don't do

We don't sell or share your information, show ads, build profiles, or use your music or voice to train AI.

7. Children

Fermata is made for singers of all ages and asks for no personal information. If you're under the age where your country allows you to agree to online services yourself, please use Fermata with a parent's or guardian's permission.

8. Your choices and rights

Because we don't keep your pages or your voice and our analytics can't identify you, there's normally nothing about you for us to hand over or delete. You can clear your recent readings from your device at any time. If you have a question or request, contact us at the address below and we'll help.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we'll update it here and change the date at the top. If we ever start collecting something new, we'll say so clearly in the app first.

2. Readings can be wrong

Reading music from a photo is hard. Fermata (and the AI it sometimes uses) can misread notes, rhythms, tempo or markings, especially on unclear, handwritten or crumpled pages. Always check important passages against your score, your teacher or your director. Warm-up scores and feedback are a practice aid, not a professional assessment.
